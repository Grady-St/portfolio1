# A3 – Parametric and FEA

## Objective
The objective of this project is to design an aluminum bar using parametric modeling and axial deflection calculations, then verify the design using Finite Element Analysis. The project compares theoretical and simulated deflection results while evaluating the stress and safety of the bar under an applied tensile load.

## Analyze
For this project, the bar will be designed from aluminum and subjected to a direct tensile load. I selected the  following dimensions and design parameters were selected

Width: 1.00 in, 
Height: 0.50 in, 
Thickness: 0.50 in, 
Applied load: 400lbf, 
MOE: 10x10^6 PSI, 
Max deflection: 0.050 in 

The selected load of 400 lbf is within the required range of 300–500 lbf, and the selected modulus of elasticity is within the required range for aluminum.

One end of the bar was fixed to represent a rigid support. A 400 lbf tensile load was applied to the opposite end in the axial direction. The same aluminum material properties used in the parametric design calculations were assigned to the model.
