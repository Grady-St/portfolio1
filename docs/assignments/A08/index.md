# A8 – Gears

## Objective
The goal of this assignment is to design a gearbox including 2 shafts, 2 gears and a common bearing journal. The design must meet certain requirements based on the outlined gear ratio, output torque and speed, shaft diameters and force requirements. The gearbox must also be easily assembled and disassembled.

## Analyze

Both the input and output shafts satisfy the handbook's recommended allowable working stress for commercial steel shafting without keyways under the assumed pure-torsion loading condition. The input shaft has a calculated maximum shear stress of 3,260 psi, below the allowable 8,000 psi, and a minimum calculated diameter of 0.0927 in. The output shaft has a calculated maximum shear stress of 1,090 psi, below the same allowable stress, and a minimum calculated diameter of 0.128 in. Therefore, the specified diameters of 0.125 in for the input shaft and 0.250 in for the output shaft are sufficient for the simplified torsional-strength analysis.

<img width="556" height="668" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-02 at 9 17 24 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73eff98a-7934-47ce-bceb-7b4abcf756d2" />

The journal bearing wall thickness was determined using the difference between the bearing's outside diameter and inside diameter, divided by two. For the input-shaft bearing, the calculation gave a nominal wall thickness of 0.0625 in. The output-shaft bearing also had a nominal wall thickness of 0.0625 in. These values establish the bearing geometry; additional analysis would be required to verify wall strength under the actual housing and loading conditions.
